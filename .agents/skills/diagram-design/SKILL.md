# Diagram Design Skill (Bespoke SVG Blueprints)

## Guidelines
1. **Human Craft & Line Quality:**
   - Use organic strokes with slight variation in dash-arrays (`stroke-dasharray="4,3"`, `stroke-linecap="round"`).
   - Use period-accurate colors: Warm Sage (`#a3e3a4`), Vintage Gold (`#d4af37`), Archival Parchment (`#e8dfc8`), and Deep Slate (`#162822`).
2. **Photographic & Technical Line Art:**
   - 1930s folding bellows press cameras (Speed Graphic style).
   - Vintage camera lenses with aperture blade rings and shutter dials.
   - Microfilm reels, spools, and exposure needle gauges.
3. **Integration:**
   - Keep SVGs inline and scalable (`viewBox`, relative sizing).
   - Ensure elements have `aria-hidden="true"` so screen readers ignore decorative flourishes.
