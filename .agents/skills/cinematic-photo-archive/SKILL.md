# Cinematic Photo Archive Skill

## Visual Patterns
1. **Photo Corner Mounts (`.photo-corner-mount`):**
   - The triangular dark leatherette / brass brackets used in 1930s photo albums to mount prints without adhesive.
2. **35mm Filmstrip Sprockets (`.filmstrip-sprockets`):**
   - Perforated borders along top/bottom of photographic artifacts and political cartoon displays mimicking negative strips and film reels.
3. **Camera Viewfinder HUD (`.viewfinder-reticle`):**
   - Corner framing brackets `┌ ┐ └ ┘` with center crosshairs `+` representing a photographer framing historical reality through a 1930s rangefinder.
4. **Archival Press & Film Stamps (`.contact-sheet-stamp`):**
   - Technical metadata stamps: `[ 35MM PANCHROMATIC · EXP. 1930 · f/4.5 · 1/50 SEC ]`.
